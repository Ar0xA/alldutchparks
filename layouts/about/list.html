About Me

Hi, I'm Art a.k.a. PD3AN — a Netherlands-based amateur radio operator that needed a kick in the ass to go outdoors, setting up a station somewhere quiet, and putting Dutch parks and nature reserves on the air.

What started as a sit-indoor hobby turned into a proper project: this site tracks every Parks on the Air activation I log, together with photos and field notes from each trip, so fellow activators and hunters can see what a park is actually like before they visit.

My POTA Gear

  • Transceiver: FTX-1 Optima
  • Antenna: 5.2m vertical whip with 16 ground radials.
  • Power: 5 to 100 W

The Challenge

The Netherlands has 325 official POTA references spread across all twelve provinces — from national parks to small municipal nature reserves. The goal behind this site is simple: activate every single one of them, at least once, and document the journey along the way.

Follow live progress on the Parks page or see it plotted on the Map.

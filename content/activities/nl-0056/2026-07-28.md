Sunny and hot sweaty afternoon activation out in Gelderland activating **NL-0056 (Bergherbos Park)**!

### Station Setup
* **Rig:** FTX-1 on its battery
* **Antenna:** Vertical whip with 16 radials on the ground
* **Mode:** FT8
* **Power:** 5 Watts

### Activation Report
The trip to the pin drop was short and easy. Or it would have been if I hadn't forgotten the chair in my car. It was a nice open space along a path where I had no issues setting up the antenna and area. A few people were interested and had a small chat. Few people with loose dogs though, carefull of that. Lots of dragonflies around too, it was very beautiful.

<div class="warning-box">
<strong>⚠️ Warning:</strong> It does have some of those blood sucking horseflies around. Keep an eye on uncovered arms, legs and necks.
</div>

Conditions on 20m were said solid today. Yeah, they **lied**. I had issues getting out there despite getting spotted around Europe, I had quite a few missed QSO's. It also didn't help I did bring my LiFePo4 battery, but forgot to check the FTX-1 cable. I had to go on the normal battery so ended up at 5W instead of the projected 10.

I had set RRR because of being QRP, and it was needed. After nearly an hour I only had 6 QSO's done. Around 17:50+02:00 I switched to 40m. That was definately the right thing to do. because Europe was smoking! In about 30 mins I had 11 aditional confirmed contacts making me end up at **16 QSOs** logged.

Sunny Saturday lunchtime activation out in Overijssel activating **NL-0052 (Buurserzand Provincial Park)**!

### Station Setup
* **Rig:** FTX-1 on car battery
* **Antenna:** Moonraker AMPRO 20M
* **Mode:** FT8
* **Power:** 8 Watts

### Activation Report
The weather for a change wasn't thunderstorm or mindbreaking heat, so I decided to hit the park I missed before, linked to the nearby [NL-0053](/activities/nl-0053/).

The parking spot is not very large but nicely tree covered and thus in the shade. Walking towards the pin-drop is going to be about 10 minutes, it starts off nice and wide but lateron the path gets extremely narrow. Think 30 centimeters, with maybe a meter free on either side. Close to the pin-drop there is just no space, way to narrow and you can't get off the path here without damaging sensitive flora.

However as you walk from the parkingspot towards the pin-drop on the Rietschot weg, you pass a large farmer-cultivated field that would work great as an activation spot. 

The area I saw itself is, honestly a little disappointing. Unlike its southward friend, much of the areas are wired off. I get it, but doesn't make for a great nature feel. The obviously farmer cultivated fields dotted around the area take away some of the beauty.

Having recently ordered a 20 and 40 meter band "oh crap the weather sucks"-mobile car antenna, I figured I might was well give them a twirl and see how they work. They tuned fine, but hey, so does a dummyload.

I have to admit, I did expect less for the 30 euro per antenna I paid for the Moonraker AMPRO's. The 20 meter one went solidly at an SWR 1.0 and at 8W I ended up covering all of Europe, and the south coast of NA.

QSO's went fine, it's always tempting to try see if you can reach those 5000+ km stations you see if you also see you get spotted around that area, but sadly that didn't really pan out this time. I ended up doing **10 QSO's** in 45 minutes! Not bad at all.

A very attractive park with cultivated farm fields in between. Easy for activation, takes away from the park's beauty. The weather was great, and the new antenna performed well.

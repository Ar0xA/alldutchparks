Amazingly nice early evening around Buurse activating **NL-0089 (Witte Veen)**!

### Station Setup
* **Rig:** FT-891
* **Antenna:** Vertical whip with radials on the ground
* **Mode:** FT8
* **Power:** 50 Watts

### Activation Report
Alright so parking here is a bit odd, no formal parking spots that I could find. I googled around and found out htey suggested parking on the Gervingshoekweg. There's a house there, but also two park entrances and on the road sides, enough space for quite a few cars to park.

The walk closer to the pin-drop took me about 10 minutes, a nice stroll along hardened biking paths. Considering the beautiful weather we were having, a delight. The area does contain some wild cows so there are special fences to get into the area. The place itself where I ended up setting up was a 3-way crossroad.

Once setup I unpacked my new Yaesu ft-891 and digirig. They're am both amazingly small and it's a surprise there is no newer trancievers that small at 100W. I can see why they're so popular with POTA enthousiasts.

That said, even after testing at home I had some issues getting things working. I didn't have one of the plugs far in enough so the com-ports didn't want to show up until I figured that out. Then windows was destroying my waterfall by enabling AGC by default on the audio device. After about 15 minutes of fiddling I finally was up and running.

Wow, so nice to not work QRP. On 20m I got heard pretty much all over the world. That said, it's directly obvious that the FT-891 is less sensitive than the FTX-1 by a large margin. Not sure if I can do anything about that, but in the end I did get stations.

Due to the location, quite a few people ended up asking me what I was doing. Considering I'm no amazing conversationalist I tell them the basic POTA and ham radio story. I doubt anyone was really understanding or intrigued.

So having to the super dry ground, the SWR was a bit of a challenge and I had to work with about 1:2. Doable, but it made the power and ALC a bit twitchy when switching around the frequencies. However due to the power of.....50W of power, it took just 30 minutes to make 10 contacts on 20 meter.

Checking the time and figuring a -why not-, I checked Gridgtracker2 and saw that 40m was "fair", but tuned it anyway with the coil. Ended up at nearly the same SWR as on 20m, a bit around 1:2. Maybe the FT-891 is just more sensitive?

40m got me a few nice contacts. But also a few that didn't get completed. 

After that I decided to head home and enjoyed the quiet and beautiful walk back to the car.

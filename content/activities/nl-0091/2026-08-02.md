Bit too sunny sunday activation out in Gelderland for **NL-0091 (Rijntakken Provincial Park)**!

### Station Setup
* **Rig:** FTX-1 on LiFePo4 battery
* **Antenna:** 5.2m whip on the ground with radials
* **Mode:** FT8
* **Power:** 8 Watts

### Activation Report
So this is totally a misnomer. This is not a park, its a gigantic area covering various three rivers, their banks and adjectant flora stretching across three provinces. It has three main areas:
- Gelderse Poort where the rhine river enters the Netherlands (going under Arnhem)
- Uiterwaarden IJssel flowercovered fields and forrests across the IJssel
- Uiterwaarden Neder-Rijn, nature area with steep banks and open waters

You can see the whole map of the covered area here: https://www.natura2000.nl/gebieden/gelderland/rijntakken/rijntakken-kaart

Obviously though, I wanted to activate near the pin-drop. While only a technicallity it's what I want to do. This was near the Badweg, an area just south of Arnhem that has quite a few recreational water bodies where people are allowed to swim. The parking is large near the artifical lake, but the road gets super narrow along the dead end. 

Parking near the pin drop can be done carefully on the side of the very narrow road. If you get oncomming traffic both of you are going to have to dodge into the roadside.

Seeing as the road was way too narrow for my car further along the Badweg, I parked in an obviously previously used roadside parking spot. Walking toward the pin drop took about 10 minutes along the road but I couldn't actually get very close because it's inside the protected area. So I walked back and setup near the car in the shade.

Using my 5.2 whip with ground radials (still too lazy to setup my diy, not really, pota performer atenna) I started on 20m since that's the easiest. Battery next to my FTX-1 I could get about 8w before ALC would hit. I'll take it! 

The moment I started, there were like 6 other POTA stations. Sadly as hard as I tried, I couldnt make park-to-park and started doing my own CQ. ER1TX got close but being QRP, I had it set on RRR and we never got it finished.

QSO contacts started slow, despite getting spotted all over Europe and quite far into North America. In the end it took about an hour only to get the 10 contacts I needed. It would have been faster, but the pota.app page refused to accept my QSO with NF3R. 

I stayed a little longer and made a few more contacts on 20 before deciding to gamble on trying 10m. The predictor said "fair", and 10m is one of my favourite bands. I got mostly spotted in Spain, and one odd duck station in Zambia. I tried for a bit, but only managed 2 QSO's in Spain.

Getting late, I decided to wrap it up, instead of trying for 40m.

Great afternoon out in Gelderland activating **NL-0084 (Korenburgerveen - Vragenderveen Provincial Park)**!

### Station Setup
* **Rig:** Ultra-portable QRP transceiver
* **Antenna:** Vertical whip with elevated radials on a tripod
* **Mode:** FT8
* **Power:** 10 Watts

### Activation Report
Conditions on 20m and 40m were solid today. Set up near the edge of the nature reserve and started calling CQ on 20m FT8. Within 30 minutes, logged a steady stream of callers across Europe.

Wrapped up the activation with **42 QSOs** in the log, easily clearing the 10-QSO threshold to make NL-0084 official!

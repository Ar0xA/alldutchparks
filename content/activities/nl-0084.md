Great afternoon out in Wintersijk activating **NL-0084 (Korenburgerveen - Vragenderveen Provincial Park)**!

### Station Setup
* **Rig:** FTX1-Optima
* **Antenna:** Vertical whip with radials on the ground
* **Mode:** FT8
* **Power:** 50 Watts

### Activation Report
Conditions on 20m and 40m were solid around lunchtime. Even 10m opened up a bit there. Set up together with <a href= "https://www.qrz.com/db/PA2PWM" target="_blank" >PA2PWM</a> along the road at a picnic bench. The weather was rather cloudy and we did get a little drizzle now and then but nothing an umbrella wouldnt fix. Later on the sun was shining.

QSO's came in easy for me blasting a good 50W over FT8, though PA2PWM had issues with his trancievers and digirig combination.

Wrapped up the activation with **36 QSOs** in the log, a respectable amount for the time we spend there.

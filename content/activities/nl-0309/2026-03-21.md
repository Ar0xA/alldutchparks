Great afternoon out in Ulft being the first activator for **NL-0309 (Korenburgerveen - Vragenderveen Provincial Park)**!

### Station Setup
* **Rig:** FTX1
* **Antenna:** Vertical whip with radials on the ground
* **Mode:** FT8
* **Power:** 5 Watts

### Activation Report
First time going out with just QRP. I had to park along the road, the area is basically just a small forest area with a small hill in the middle. The hike up the hill was short, all in all it's pretty small. The small wooden bench up top was large enough to setup my gear on.

Conditions were amazing, 20m was blasting away! I couldn't get 10m tuned up though, probably issues with the radials. It was also very quiet around, no noticable QRM or even people. The weather was very nice also.

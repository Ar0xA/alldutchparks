Wet sunday lunchtime activation out in Overijssel activating **NL-0053 (Haaksbergseveen Provincial Park)**!

### Station Setup
* **Rig:** FTX-1 on its battery
* **Antenna:** Vertical whip with 16 radials on the ground
* **Mode:** FT8
* **Power:** 5 Watts

### Activation Report
Conditions on 20m were solid today. Heard most of Europe, North-America, most of Russia and even some Japan. PSKReporter said the NA cost heard me too, but I couldn't get any contacts done, sadly.

Park bounds would allow you to activate from the parkinglot there too but where's the fun in that? There are two obvious roads, a biking path and a maintenance road. The biking path has benches along it and is hardened. The maintenance road is soft sand. I suggest hiking along the biking path, specially if you bring more gear.

The pin drop itself is in off the road so sitting exactly on that was a no go. Also it was a walking area only with little space so I walked a bit further and ended up setting up in a road toward a closed off area. This kept me off the normal path without going off the road.

The moment I setup it started to drizzle, then rain. Holding an umbrella in one hand while operating with the other worked alright, though it was not comfortable. The FTX-1 had to stay in the backpack with the laptop on my lap. Condition's stayed pretty much the same throughout the activation. 

It started to rain hard at some point, and then stopped. Checking the weather radar it said lightning and thunderstorm was incomming so I wrapped up the activation with **13 QSOs** in the log. So with some difficulty NL-0053 is officialy activated!
